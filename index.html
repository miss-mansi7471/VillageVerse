    Welcome to VillageVerse

    A tiny living village of 12 souls — each with their own needs, memories, friendships, grudges and gossip.

    🧠
    They remember
    Every chat, trade, gift and argument becomes a memory that shapes how they feel about each other.
    🗣️
    Rumours spread & twist
    Gossip passes from villager to villager and gets exaggerated — or blamed on someone else — along the way.
    📔
    They keep diaries
    Each villager writes a diary in their own voice, made from what actually happened to them.
    🌗
    Day & night
    They wake, work, trade, eat, socialise in the evening glow and go home to sleep.

    How to play

    • 🖱️ Click a villager on the map (or in the roster under it) to open their needs, relationships, memories, diary and rumours.
    • ⏸ Pause/Resume with the button or Space. Change speed with 1× 2× 4× 8× or keys 1–4.
    • 📜 The Log shows everything happening live. 🗣️ Rumours follows each piece of gossip as it changes. 📊 Village shows who likes whom.
    • 💡 Tip: run at 4× for a minute, then open someone's Diary.
